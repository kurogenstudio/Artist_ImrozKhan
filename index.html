Imroz Khan is a live performance artist, working across spoken word, poetry, storytelling and ghazals. A law student who commands narratives both in the courtroom and on stage, he is known for sharp wit, impeccable comic timing, and powerful social commentary expressed through Hindi, Urdu, Marathi and English.

With 3+ years of active performance experience, he has performed at leading platforms across the country, curated cultural spaces, written for theatre, and continues to explore new ways of using words to question, connect and create conversations.
